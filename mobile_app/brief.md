# StoryLens Mobile App Brief

## 1. What is the primary goal of this app?
The primary goal is to provide a mobile-native version of StoryLens. Users can type a simple story premise and instantly generate a cinematic movie trailer or a graphic motion comic. It aims to empower creators, storytellers, and casual users to visualize their ideas effortlessly from their phones.

## 2. What is the "Magical Moment"?
The magical moment is when the progress bar hits 100%, and the user's premise suddenly starts playing as a fully-produced, audiovisual trailer with AI-generated scenes, professional narration, and a cinematic soundtrack.

## 3. What is the preferred aesthetic?
A premium, cinematic dark mode. Deep blacks (`#06060a`), sleek glassmorphic overlays (`rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`), and golden accents (`#e8c547`) to evoke the feeling of a professional movie studio editing bay. Typography should be modern, clean, and screenwriting-inspired (like a monospaced font combined with a serif for headers).
