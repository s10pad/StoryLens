# StoryLens Mobile - Product Document

## Core Value Proposition
StoryLens Mobile brings the power of AI trailer generation to your fingertips. Instead of sitting at a desktop, users can tap out a story idea on their phone, pick a mood, and watch an entire cinematic trailer assemble itself natively on iOS or Android.

## MVP Features
1. **The Prompter**: A simple, distraction-free input screen where users describe their premise.
2. **Director's Setup**: Dropdowns or chips to select "Format" (Video vs. Graphic), "Genre", and "Tone".
3. **Generation Pipeline**: A progress screen that shows live updates as directions, scenes, audio, and video clips are generated.
4. **The Theater**: A native video player screen to watch the final assembled trailer.
5. **Share/Save**: Native iOS/Android share sheet integration to export the final `.mp4`.

## User Flows
- **Flow 1**: Launch App -> Enter Premise -> Select Options -> Tap "Generate" -> Wait on loading screen -> Watch Trailer.
